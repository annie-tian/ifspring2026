LEGO Design

Universal System

LEGO bricks are part of a universal system where bricks made decades apart still fit together. This compatibility is a key feature, allowing endless creative possibilities.

Precision

Each LEGO element is manufactured to high tolerances, often measured in hundredths of a millimeter, ensuring bricks fit snugly while remaining easy to separate.

Design Studios

The company’s primary design work occurs at LEGO’s Billund headquarters, with smaller studios in other countries adapting products for local markets. Sets range from simple models for beginners to highly detailed advanced builds.

System of Play

LEGO’s modular design philosophy supports creativity across all age groups, allowing builders to combine sets in countless ways to construct unique creations.
