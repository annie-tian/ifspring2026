LEGO in Media

Movies

LEGO has been featured in animated movies, including The Lego Movie (2014) and its sequels, as well as spin-offs like The Lego Batman Movie and The Lego Ninjago Movie. These films bring LEGO worlds to life with humor and imaginative storytelling.

Video Games

LEGO video games adapt popular themes into interactive experiences, allowing players to build, explore, and solve puzzles in virtual LEGO worlds.

TV and Online Content

LEGO content is also available on TV shows, animated specials, and online platforms. Social media and official LEGO channels share news, behind-the-scenes content, and highlight LEGO products and creative builds.
